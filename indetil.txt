1. What this application is
Purpose
A MERN (MongoDB, Express, React, Node) application to track field salespersons:
Assign locations and routes.
Capture GPS‑verified visit reports with photo proof.
Track live locations on a map.
Manage expenses and approvals.
Provide notifications and performance ratings.
Main user roles
Admin
Creates and manages salespersons.
Assigns locations (with GPS coordinates and radius).
Monitors live locations and visit status.
Reviews visit reports and expenses.
Sends notifications.
Salesperson
Logs in on mobile/desktop.
Sees daily assignments on a map.
Goes to each location, submits visit report + photos + GPS.
Submits expenses and tracks approval status.
Sees their own performance rating and stats.

4. Core backend modules
4.1 Authentication (
authController.js
, authRoutes.js, authMiddleware.js)
Login (POST /auth/login)
Finds Salesperson by email.
Verifies password with bcrypt.
Issues JWT { id, role } with expiresIn: '24h'.
Sets cookie ctoken (httpOnly, secure flags) and returns { token, user }.
Logs login activity (LoginActivity) and optionally reverse‑geocodes location.
Register (POST /auth/register)
Protected by custom header x-app-key === APP_KEY for security.
Validates email/password format.
Creates new Salesperson with role default salesperson.
Admin accounts must be promoted manually in DB (README describes this).
Logout (POST /auth/logout)
Clears cookie and logs logout activity.
Middleware
authMiddleware:
Reads JWT from Authorization: Bearer OR ctoken cookie.
Verifies with JWT_SECRET and sets req.user.
adminMiddleware:
Checks req.user.role === 'admin'.
limiter:
Rate‑limits sensitive routes (auth, sales, admin, etc.).
4.2 Salesperson operations (
salesController.js
, salesRoutes.js)
Get assignments (GET /salesperson/:id/assignments)
Validates ObjectId.
Only admin or the same salesperson can view these.
Returns sorted Assignment docs.
Upload visit & photo (POST /salesperson/upload-photo)
multer handles up to 5 images, stored under 
uploads/
.
Body includes:
assignmentId, placeId, lat, lng
Full visit report fields (company, mill details, competitor, requirement, siteVisited flags, etc.).
Steps:
Validate ownership of assignment and place.
Calculate distance between submitted GPS and assigned place (calculateDistance).
Mark visit as suspicious if distance > 100m.
Store main photoUrl + additionalPhotoUrls.
Update place fields in Assignment.places (visited, verified, visitedAt, distanceTraveled).
Update assignment.totalDistance and status.
Save:
PhotoUpload (legacy GPS + photo record).
VisitReport (full structured report).
Recalculate salesperson rating (
updateSalespersonRating
, Salesperson.calculateRating).
Create system notifications for all admins (via 
createSystemNotification
).
Send email to each admin (sendVisitCompletionEmail).
Update salesperson’s currentLocation and lastActive.
Update location (POST /salesperson/update-location)
Updates Salesperson.currentLocation and lastActive.
Get own rating (GET /salesperson/my-rating)
Returns name, email, rating, performanceStats, dateJoined, isActive.

Logs in (/auth/login).
Goes to /admin/assign:
Creates assignments for each salesperson, with date + places (GPS).
Monitors /admin/map:

Reviews visits:
/admin/visit-reports:

Reviews expenses:
/admin/expense-reports:

Salesperson rating auto‑updates; notifications and emails are sent.
Sends broadcast notifications if needed (/notifications/create).
7.2 Salesperson
Logs in on mobile browser:
Gets token & cookie.
/dashboard:
Sees today’s assignments, locations on map, and their rating.
App updates their live GPS to backend.
For each assigned place:
Clicks “Visit Location” → /visit/:assignmentId/:placeId.
Fills the full visit form, attaches one or more photos.
Submits:
Backend verifies GPS distance → Verified or Suspicious.
Visit report and photo are stored; admin is notified.
After the day:
Fills expenses in /expenses/create with bill photos.
Tracks approval and stats in /expenses/view.
Checks notifications in /notifications:
Sees visit verification status, expense approvals, admin messages
8. Vendor pitch guide – how to present this solution

Goal
Explain clearly to a vendor or decision-maker why this Sales Tracker is valuable for their business, and how it can be implemented with low risk and high return.

Who you are talking to
Typically you are speaking to:
- Business owner, director, or sales head of a company with field sales team.
- Sometimes IT head or operations manager who will check feasibility.

60-second elevator pitch (short version you can memorize)
“We have built a Sales Tracker system that gives you full visibility of your field sales team. Admins can assign GPS-based locations, salespeople check in with photos and automatic GPS verification, and you get clean visit reports, live tracking, and expense control in a single dashboard. It reduces fake visits, controls leakages in expenses, and gives you real data to manage performance – without forcing your team to learn a complicated system.”

Opening the conversation – problem first
Start with problems they already feel:
- How do you currently track whether field visits actually happen at the customer location?
- Do you rely only on WhatsApp photos and verbal updates from salespeople?
- Do you face fake or duplicate claims for travel and daily expenses?
- When a salesperson leaves the company, how easily can you check what visits they actually did?

Then connect those pains to the solution:
- Our system replaces manual WhatsApp/Excel tracking with a proper platform.
- Every visit has GPS verification and photo proof so you know exactly what happened.
- Expenses are digital, easy to approve, and linked to salesperson performance.

How to position the product (benefits language)
Focus on business benefits, not only features:
- Control and transparency
  - Live map of all salespersons with real-time locations.
  - GPS-verified visits reduce fake check-ins and save management time.
- Higher quality field execution
  - Standard visit report form ensures every visit captures complete data.
  - Suspicious visits (location mismatch) are clearly flagged for review.
- Expense optimization
  - Digital expense submissions with photos of bills.
  - Approval workflow + Excel export for accounts team.
- Better performance management
  - Automatic rating system based on approved visits and expenses.
  - Data for incentives, appraisals, and coaching low performers.
- Compliance and audit trail
  - Complete history of who visited which client, when, and with which proof.

How to structure a 20–30 minute pitch meeting
1. Warm-up (2–3 minutes)
   - Introduce yourself and your company.
   - Ask how they are currently managing field sales tracking.

2. Understand their current process (5 minutes)
   - Listen to how they assign routes, collect reports, and approve expenses.
   - Note specific pain points (e.g., fake visits, missing data, late reports).

3. Present the concept (5 minutes)
   - Use simple story:
     - “Admin creates assignments for each salesperson with GPS locations.”
     - “Salesperson opens mobile-friendly dashboard, goes to customer, clicks Visit, takes photo, submits report.”
     - “System checks GPS, marks verified or suspicious, and updates dashboard.”
     - “Expenses and notifications are all in the same system.”

4. Demo flow (8–10 minutes) – what to show
   - Show login as salesperson:
     - Dashboard with assignments and map.
     - How to open a visit, upload photo, and submit report.
   - Show login as admin:
     - Admin dashboard with stats (total visits, suspicious uploads).
     - Live locations map.
     - Visit reports list + filters + CSV export.
     - Expense reports screen: pending/approved/rejected, Excel export.
     - Notifications screen: how admins get alerts for suspicious visits and expenses.

5. Talk about implementation (5 minutes)
   - Setup steps:
     - Host backend and frontend (can use your existing Render/Netlify/Vercel flow).
     - Create admin accounts.
     - Import or create salespersons list.
     - Train admins (1–2 sessions) and field team (short demo).
   - Timeline:
     - Pilot with 5–10 salespersons in 1–2 weeks.
     - Full rollout in 4–6 weeks depending on team size.
   - IT requirements:
     - Just a browser and smartphones with GPS + internet.
     - No need for each user to install a heavy native app.

6. Commercial and ROI angle
Do not only quote price; talk about savings:
- Reduction in fake or unverified visits.
- Better control of daily travel/food/fuel expenses.
- Less time spent on manual follow-up and Excel consolidation.
- Better conversion because managers can coach based on real data.
You can say, for example:
- “If you prevent even 2–3 fake visits or wrong expense claims per salesperson per month, the system pays for itself.”

Handling common objections
1) “Our sales team may resist or feel monitored.”
- Response: Position it as a tool to prove genuine hard work.
- Highlight rating and visibility benefits for good performers.

2) “Will it be difficult to use for our team?”
- Response: Show simple mobile screens.
- Explain that basic actions are just: Login → See map → Visit → Take photo → Submit.

3) “We already use WhatsApp/Excel, why change?”
- Response: WhatsApp is unstructured and unsearchable; Excel is manual.
- This system gives structured data, GPS verification, exports, and dashboards in one place.

Short sample script you can customize
“Right now, your sales team is doing a lot of hard work in the field, but you cannot see everything clearly. Some visits are genuine, some reports are incomplete, and expenses often come as WhatsApp photos and loose bills.

We built a Sales Tracker that solves this in a practical way. Admins assign locations with GPS, salespersons open a simple mobile screen, visit the customer, click Visit, take a photo, and submit a detailed report. The system automatically checks GPS, flags suspicious visits, and updates your dashboard.

On the admin side you get:
- Live map of who is where.
- Verified vs suspicious visits.
- Complete visit history and reports in Excel.
- Full control over expenses with photo proofs and approval flow.

Implementation is simple – we can start with a small pilot team in a couple of weeks, and if you like the results, scale to your full field force. The goal is not to police your team, but to reward genuine work and reduce leakages in visits and expenses.”

How to close the meeting
- Ask for a small pilot: “Can we start with 5–10 salespersons for one month?”
- Agree on:
  - Pilot start date.
  - Who will be admin champion on their side.
  - What success metrics they care about (e.g., verified visits %, expense turnaround time).
